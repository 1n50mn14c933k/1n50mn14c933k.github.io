Users remain responsible for their files, storage devices, and backups. Before using operations that rename, move, reorganize, or otherwise modify large numbers of files, users should maintain a current and independent backup of the original data.

Guided Mode defaults to COPY, leaving source files in place. MOVE requires an explicit choice in Advanced Mode and may remove source files after moving them. Keep an independent backup and verify the destination before bulk operations.
