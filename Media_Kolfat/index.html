Media management software

Bring your scattered memories back into order.

Photos and videos tend to end up everywhere — on your PC, laptop, phone, tablet, USB drive, flash storage, external disks, and cloud folders. After a while, it becomes difficult to remember when something was taken, which device it came from, or where a specific memory is stored.

What if you could organize all of it in one simple workflow?

Media Kolfat helps you collect media from different sources and turn a scattered library into a clear, structured archive. Add or connect your folders, choose how you want your media organized, and let Media Kolfat prepare a new folder structure for you.

Your photos and videos can be arranged into meaningful folders such as Year → Month → Day, with additional organization based on available information such as camera or device name. Instead of manually sorting thousands of files, you get a clean structure that is easier to browse, understand, and keep for the long term.
