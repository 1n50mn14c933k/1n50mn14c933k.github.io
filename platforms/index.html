Privacy and transparency

This survey does not use a voting database, signup, cookies, tracking scripts or automatic vote submission. We manually tally emails received at contact@kolfat.app. Please send one response per person.

Email is not anonymous. If you send a response, your email address and usual email metadata are shared with your email provider and ours. Don't include sensitive information. We do not use survey responses to subscribe you to marketing emails.

No email app configured? Write to contact@kolfat.app with the subject MEDIA KOLFAT Platform Interest and your choice: macOS, Linux, or Both. See our privacy policy.
