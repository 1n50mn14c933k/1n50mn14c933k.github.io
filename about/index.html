About KOLFAT

KOLFAT builds practical software for people who want more order, clarity, and control over their digital media without turning a simple task into a complicated system.

Explore MEDIA KOLFATDownload for Windows

Order from chaos

KOLFAT started with a familiar problem: photos and videos spread across PCs, phones, tablets, USB drives, external disks, backups, and cloud folders. The more memories you collect, the harder it becomes to understand where everything belongs.

The goal is straightforward: build tools that make large media libraries easier to organize, review, and preserve.

MEDIA KOLFAT

MEDIA KOLFAT is the first KOLFAT application. It is a Windows photo and video organizer with guided and advanced workflows, copy and move options, duplicate finding, library browsing, activity history, photo rotation, and local processing.

The core application is available free through the Microsoft Store.

Privacy-conscious by design

MEDIA KOLFAT is designed around local media management. Your normal organization workflow works with files and folders you control instead of requiring you to hand your library to a remote service.
