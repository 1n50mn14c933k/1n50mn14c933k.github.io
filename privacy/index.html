Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Kolfat is committed to keeping the handling of personal information clear and minimal. Media Kolfat is designed primarily to help you organize your own photos and videos.

2. Media Kolfat and your files

Media Kolfat may access folders, photos, videos, file metadata, timestamps, camera information, device information stored in media files, and other file properties that you choose to make available to the application.

This access is used to analyze, organize, classify, preview, and arrange your media library according to the features you use.

3. Local processing

Media Kolfat is designed to perform its core media-management operations locally on your device. Your photos and videos are not sold to third parties.

If a future feature requires an online service, cloud processing, account sign-in, or another external service, this policy will be updated to explain that processing before or when such a feature is introduced.

4. Website data

When you visit kolfat.app, standard technical information may be processed by the hosting, DNS, security, and delivery infrastructure required to operate the website. This can include information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, requested page, and request time.

5. Cookies and optional website analytics

With your explicit consent, kolfat.app uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand page views, approximate location, browser and device information, and clicks to the Microsoft Store or GitHub release pages. This helps us improve the website and understand interest in MEDIA KOLFAT. Website analytics do not access your local photos or files.

GA4 scripts are not loaded and no GA4 analytics requests are sent unless you accept optional analytics. Analytics cookies such as _ga may be set after consent. Advertising storage, personalized ads, and Google advertising signals are disabled. You may decline or withdraw your choice at any time via the “Privacy settings” button on the website. Withdrawing consent stops further tracking but cannot undo data already processed.

Google may process analytics data outside the European Economic Area subject to applicable safeguards. Retention is governed by the GA4 property settings. For Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy. Privacy questions: Contact KOLFAT.

6. Contact information

If you contact Kolfat by email, the information you provide in your message will be used to respond to your request, provide support, investigate issues, or communicate about Kolfat products.

7. Data sharing

Kolfat does not sell personal data.

Information may be processed by service providers only where necessary to operate the website, deliver software, provide security, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide support, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, or resolve disputes.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect Kolfat systems and information. No method of storage, transmission, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children

Kolfat products are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how data is handled.

Requests can be sent to Contact KOLFAT.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Kolfat products, website functionality, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact: Contact KOLFAT

Media Kolfat: this policy is intended to cover the current privacy model of the application and website. It should be reviewed whenever new cloud, account, analytics, advertising, AI, or online-processing features are added.
