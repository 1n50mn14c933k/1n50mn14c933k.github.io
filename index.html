03 / Security baseline

Build it so it can be trusted and rebuilt.

The lab is a learning environment, but the operating principles are deliberately based on production practices.

LinuxProxmoxDockerAnsibleUniFiWireGuardNetBirdauthentikGitHubBackup & DR
01
No secrets in public GitPasswords, tokens, keys, private addressing, customer data, and sensitive configuration stay out.
02
Least privilege by defaultUsers, services, containers, integrations, and automation receive only the access they need.
03
Back up before changingSnapshots, backups, and rollback planning come before potentially disruptive work.
04
Monitor before trustingLogs, health checks, alerts, and observability are part of the deployment, not an afterthought.
05
Document for recoveryDocumentation should explain what exists, why it exists, how it is secured, and how to rebuild it.
