Organize Your Photos with Guided COPY

Prepare a safe COPY job and check the results.

Guided COPY is the recommended first organization method. Have an independent backup and use a separate destination with enough space.

Follow along in the app

  1. STEP 01

    With Guided selected, open Organize Photos & Videos. Review the Choose File Types dialog and its scanned, selected and excluded counts.

  2. STEP 02

    Choose Deselect All if you want to rebuild the selection. With no type selected, Continue is unavailable. Select at least one supported type before proceeding.

  3. STEP 03

    Choose Select All to include all discovered types. This demonstration then selects 17 files, including its optional text file.

  4. STEP 04

    Clear the .txt checkbox to exclude the optional text file. Verify 17 scanned, 16 selected and one excluded before continuing.

  5. STEP 05

    Choose Continue. Check Selected Folder and its complete path before configuring the organization pattern.

  6. STEP 06

    Choose Year / Month / Day in Pattern and close the list to confirm it. Review the organization plan and category counts before selecting a destination. This plan uses the untouched original 16-media source.

  7. STEP 07

    Choose Change Location when you want a specific destination. The native Windows picker requires an existing folder; keep it separate from your source and backup.

  8. STEP 08

    Enter or navigate to your intended destination and choose Select Folder. The demonstration uses Copy Results. MEDIA KOLFAT adds its MEDIA KOLFAT Organized Media root inside the selected folder.

Finished? Compare your results with the screenshots above. The example numbers and paths in the screenshots are from a small demonstration library, not a promise of identical results on your PC.
