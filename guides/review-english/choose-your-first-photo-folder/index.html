Add Your First Photo Folder

Choose a folder, check its contents, and confirm that MEDIA KOLFAT scanned it.

Start here if your pictures are already in a folder on your PC, USB drive, or external disk. Adding a source indexes its supported media; it does not organize or move your original files.

Follow along in the app

  1. STEP 01

    Choose Choose Photos & Videos Manually. In the folder picker, review Current folder, Locations, Folders and Photos & Videos in this folder before selecting anything.

  2. STEP 02

    Choose Go. Check the displayed folder path and the nested folder names. A parent folder can show no media directly even though its subfolders contain supported files.

  3. STEP 03

    Open a known subfolder to review its visible previews. This example opens 2024 Holiday and shows six generated JPEG images.

  4. STEP 04

    Use Up one folder to return to the parent folder when you want to include the full nested collection. Verify Current folder again.

  5. STEP 05

    Choose Select This Folder. Read the proposed folder name and full path on Ready to add this folder. Do not continue if the displayed source is different from the folder you intended.

  6. STEP 06

    Start the offered scan and wait for the source card to show Ready and a successful scan time. The first demonstrated source contains 16 supported media files; its text file is not indexed as media.

Finished? Compare your results with the screenshots above. The example numbers and paths in the screenshots are from a small demonstration library, not a promise of identical results on your PC.
