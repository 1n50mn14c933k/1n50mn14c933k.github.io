Find Exact Duplicate Photos

Scan for identical files, inspect groups and choose what to keep.

This tutorial is about exact duplicates, not photos that merely look similar. Review the duplicate groups before deciding whether anything should be removed.

Follow along in the app

  1. STEP 01

    Open Duplicate Finder after scanning the source. Review how many photos and videos are ready to check, then choose Find Duplicates.

  2. STEP 02

    Wait for Duplicate scan complete. Review the totals before making any decisions: the initial demonstration found three groups, six duplicate files and about 16.9 MB potentially reclaimable.

  3. STEP 03

    Scroll through Exact Duplicate Group entries. Compare the file names, locations and sizes. Open Folder can help locate a listed file; browsing a group does not remove it.

  4. STEP 04

    For a larger collection, watch Finding exact duplicates and the number of candidate files checked. The larger generated test contains 544 catalog items. The tested build shows Stop Scan during this operation.

Finished? Compare your results with the screenshots above. The example numbers and paths in the screenshots are from a small demonstration library, not a promise of identical results on your PC.
