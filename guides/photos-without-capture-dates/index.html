Missing EXIF dates? Organize photos without guessing

A practical approach to photos with no reliable capture date, screenshots and edited files without mixing up your memories.

Safety first: keep an independent backup of important originals. Organization is never a replacement for backup.

1. Know the difference between dates

EXIF capture time, filesystem creation time and modified time are not interchangeable. Copying a file can change filesystem timestamps without changing when the photo was taken.

2. Identify files with no trusted capture date

Screenshots, exported edits, chat downloads and old scans often lack reliable original capture metadata. Treat them as a separate group for review.

3. Avoid inventing a date

Do not silently assign a current or modification date as a historical capture date. Keep uncertain items identifiable until you can validate them.

4. Check sources and metadata carefully

Review original folders, device context, timezone offsets and incorrect camera clocks. Preserve the untouched file before changing metadata in another tool.

5. Inspect the organized results

Review the destination, unknown-date files and reports. Spot-check photos against their real events and retain backups before manually correcting any dates.
