COPY or MOVE? Choose the safest way to organize your photos

Understand Guided COPY, deliberate Advanced MOVE, and the checks to make before changing your photo library.

Safety first: keep an independent backup of important originals. Organization is never a replacement for backup.

1. Protect your original library

Back up irreplaceable photos to another disk or backup system. Decide where the organized library should go before changing any source folders.

2. Begin with Guided COPY

Guided Mode focuses on COPY: it creates organized output while keeping your original files in place. This is the safer first choice for a new collection.

3. Use MOVE only when you really mean it

On Windows, Advanced Mode offers explicit MOVE in addition to COPY. Moving changes the source location. Linux beta keeps MOVE disabled, and macOS is not yet publicly released.

4. Try a small mixed sample

Test a small batch with photographs, videos, duplicates and files with uncertain dates. Keep the destination on a verified writable drive with enough space.

5. Verify before cleaning up

Check the output, inspect sample files, compare counts and review reports or Activity History. Do not delete originals just because a progress bar finished.
