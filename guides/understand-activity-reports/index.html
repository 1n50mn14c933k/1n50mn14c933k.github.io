Understand Activity History and Reports

Learn what successful, skipped and failed operations mean, and how to verify an organized library before cleanup.

Important: organization does not replace an independent backup.

1. Locate the operation record

Open Activity History or the report for the completed job. Keep the original source and destination paths available for comparison.

2. Separate copied, moved, skipped and failed items

A completed job may contain exceptions. Treat skipped items and errors as investigation tasks rather than assuming the entire library was processed.

3. Compare the planned result with the output

Compare source counts, destination folders and output totals. Some sources may be excluded or categorized separately; investigate differences.

4. Open a sample of actual media files

Check random photos, long videos, files with uncertain dates and important originals. A reported copy is not the same as a verified working file.

5. Keep the report before any cleanup

Save the report with the backup and only consider removing old sources after checking the files and understanding any outstanding errors.
