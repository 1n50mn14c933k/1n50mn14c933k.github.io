MEDIA KOLFAT USER GUIDE

Simple Steps
for Your Photos

This guide helps you use MEDIA KOLFAT step by step. No technical knowledge is required.

Before You Start

  • Make sure your computer is turned on.
  • Connect your USB drive, camera, or external storage if your photos are there.
  • Keep a backup of important memories.

1. Open MEDIA KOLFAT

① Open the Windows Start menu.
② Search for MEDIA KOLFAT.
③ Click the application icon.

2. Performance Check

MEDIA KOLFAT checks your computer so it can work smoothly.

  • Click Start Check.
  • Wait until the check finishes.
  • Continue to the next step.

3. Add Your Photos

Recommended: Scan Computer Automatically

MEDIA KOLFAT searches common photo locations for you.

Choose Folder Manually

Use this when you know where your photos are stored, such as a USB drive or external hard drive.

4. Photos Health Check

MEDIA KOLFAT checks your pictures and shows which files can be used safely.

  • Good photos
  • Photos without useful dates
  • Files needing attention

5. Organize Photos by Date

MEDIA KOLFAT helps create organized folders based on your photos.

Example:

Photos
 └── 2026
     └── July
         └── Vacation

Review the changes before continuing. Your confirmation is required before organizing.

6. Duplicate Finder

Find extra copies of the same photo and save storage space.

  • Start the scan.
  • Review the results.
  • Select unwanted copies.
  • Move them to the Recycle Bin.

MEDIA KOLFAT protects your last remaining copy of a file.

7. Library

Browse and view your organized media in one place.

8. Settings

  • Confirm before organizing: asks before making changes.
  • Backup reminder: helps protect your memories.
  • Reset Settings: returns preferences to the beginning.

Common Questions

"Does MEDIA KOLFAT upload my photos?"

No. MEDIA KOLFAT processes your files locally on your computer.

"Why does a photo have no date?"

Some photos do not contain date information. This can happen with screenshots or copied images.

"Is my data safe?"

Always keep backups of important files. MEDIA KOLFAT includes safety steps before file changes.
