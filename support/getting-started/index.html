Step 1 — Open MEDIA KOLFAT

Click the Windows Start button → Search MEDIA KOLFAT → Open the app.

A welcome screen will guide you.

📁

Step 2 — Find Your Photos

Choose the easy option:

Scan Computer Automatically
MEDIA KOLFAT searches common photo locations for you.

Choose Folder Manually
Select your Pictures folder, USB drive, or external disk.

❤️

Step 3 — Photos Health Check

MEDIA KOLFAT checks your photos and shows if everything is OK.

Green means ready. Attention messages explain what needs review.

📅

Step 4 — Organize Photos

MEDIA KOLFAT can arrange photos by date so they are easier to find.

Find copies of the same picture and save storage space.
